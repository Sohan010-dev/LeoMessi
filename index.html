An era defined by impossible magic, brought to a poetic close.

Football is a game of moments, but for over two decades, Lionel Andrés Messi turned it into an endless exhibition of art. From a boy in Rosario facing overwhelming physical odds, to the undisputed king of the sport.

In October 2026, the boots that painted a thousand masterpieces were finally unlaced. This is the timeline of the greatest story ever told on grass.

The Timeline

1987

The Boy from Rosario

Born in Santa Fe, Argentina. Diagnosed with a growth hormone deficiency, his sheer talent was too vast for his small frame to contain. A grandmother's unwavering belief sparked the journey.

1998

The Greatest Obstacle

At age 11, Messi was diagnosed with Growth Hormone Deficiency (GHD). His immense talent was trapped inside a body that refused to grow. The exorbitant cost of daily injections threatened to end his footballing dream before it even began, as local Argentine clubs hesitated to cover the medical bills.

2000 — 2021

The Blaugrana Symphony

FC Barcelona agreed to pay for his medical treatment, and a contract on a napkin birthed an empire. He debuted at 17 and went on to redefine the club, delivering countless iconic moments—including holding his shirt aloft at the Bernabéu.

672 Goals · 4 UCLs

2022

Completing Football

The ghost of Maracanã was buried in Lusail. In the greatest final ever played, Messi willed Argentina to their third star, lifting the World Cup and cementing his status as the eternal GOAT.
